Privacy and Terms

Aira is a free AI assistant made by Prince Yadav. This page explains in simple words what happens to your information.

Your Chats

If you do not log in, your chat is kept only in your own browser. Aira's server does not save it. Press New to erase it, or clear your browser data.

If you log in, your account details (see "Logging in with Google" below) and your saved chats are stored on Aira's server so you can open them again. You can delete your account and all saved chats yourself from the Account button.

When you send a message, it travels through Aira's server to an AI service that writes the answer. That AI service handles your text under its own rules. Please do not type passwords, bank details, ID numbers or other private information.

Logging in with Google

You log in with your Google account. Google asks you to choose the account and sign in on Google's own page; Aira never sees your Google password. Google tells Aira your email address (which Google has verified), your name, your profile picture and a unique Google ID. Aira stores these, the date you joined and the time of your last login, so it can recognise your account and show your name. Your saved chats, projects, documents, tasks and other items are tied to that account and are private to you.

If you delete your account, this information and your saved items are erased. Feedback you sent keeps its message text, but your name, email and WhatsApp number are removed from it.

Photos, Web Search and Website Builder

A photo you attach is shrunk in your browser and sent to the AI service for that one question. It is not saved by Aira. When "Search web" is on, your message is sent to a web search service (DuckDuckGo) and the results are shown to the AI. Websites made in builder mode run in a locked sandbox; do not put private information in them.

Feedback

When you are logged in and send feedback, the owner sees your message, star rating, the time, and automatically your Google name and verified email, so they know who wrote it. You can also add a WhatsApp number (with country code) if you want a reply there. This is optional, Google does not provide your number, and you never need to share one to log in or use Aira. A number is stored only if you type it and tick the consent box. If you are not logged in, the feedback is anonymous apart from any email you choose to type.

Usage Limits

To keep Aira free, the number of messages per person per day is limited. To do this, your internet address is turned into a random code and used only to count messages. The address itself is not stored.

AI Can Be Wrong

Aira is an AI. It can make mistakes, so check important facts. Do not use it for medical, legal or financial decisions.

Contact

Use the Feedback button on the main page to ask a question or request that your feedback be removed.
