          Questions people ask

          Is Aira free?

          Yes. To keep it free for everyone, each person has a daily message limit. You can see how many you have left on the Home page.

          Are my chats saved?

          If you are not logged in, your chat stays only in this browser. If you create an account, your chats are saved online and you can open them from any device.

          I forgot my password.

          There is no reset by email yet. Send feedback and say which email you used, and the owner can set a new password for you.

          How do I make a website?

          Open AI Chat, choose the Website builder mode, and describe the site. When Aira writes the code, press Preview to see it or Download to save the file.

          Aira said something wrong.

          Aira is an AI and can make mistakes. Check important facts, and do not rely on it for medical, legal or money decisions.

          How do I delete my data?

          Log in, press Account, and choose Delete account. This erases your account and all saved chats.
