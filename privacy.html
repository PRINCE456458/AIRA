Privacy and terms

Aira is a free AI assistant made by Prince Yadav. This page explains in simple words what happens to your information.

Your chats

If you do not log in, your chat is kept only in your own browser. Aira's server does not save it. Press New to erase it, or clear your browser data.

If you create an account, your email, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and your saved chats are stored on Aira's server so you can open them again. You can delete your account and all saved chats yourself from the Account button. There is no password reset by email.

When you send a message, it travels through Aira's server to an AI service that writes the answer. That AI service handles your text under its own rules. Please do not type passwords, bank details, ID numbers or other private information.

Photos, web search and website builder

A photo you attach is shrunk in your browser and sent to the AI service for that one question. It is not saved by Aira. When "Search web" is on, your message is sent to a web search service (DuckDuckGo) and the results are shown to the AI. Websites made in builder mode run in a locked sandbox; do not put private information in them.

Feedback

If you send feedback, the message, your star rating and the optional email or WhatsApp number you typed are stored so the owner can read them and reply. Leave the contact box empty if you do not want a reply.

Usage limits

To keep Aira free, the number of messages per person per day is limited. To do this, your internet address is turned into a random code and used only to count messages. The address itself is not stored.

AI can be wrong

Aira is an AI. It can make mistakes, so check important facts. Do not use it for medical, legal or financial decisions.

Contact

Use the Feedback button on the main page to ask a question or request that your feedback be removed.
